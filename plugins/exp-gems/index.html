Overview

What EXP-Gems adds

EXP-Gems gives RPG and fantasy servers a tangible way to reward progression. Players can craft or obtain gems that improve experience gains and provide a more visible upgrade path than invisible permissions.

RPG servers and communities that want experience boosts to exist as collectible items.
