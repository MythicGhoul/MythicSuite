Full overview

Make useful vanilla items craftable.

Uncraftable adds crafting paths for selected vanilla items that are normally limited, non-renewable or unavailable at a crafting table. The goal is to improve long-term survival access without installing a large custom crafting framework.

It is particularly useful in limited worlds, long-running servers and environments where exploration structures have already been heavily looted.

Best suited toSmall, long-running or border-limited survival worlds that need carefully balanced renewable items.

How it works

Renewable access through familiar crafting

Configured recipes are registered when the server starts and appear through the normal crafting system. Players do not need a custom workbench or special command to use them.

Administrators choose which formerly uncraftable items should become renewable and balance the recipe costs around the local economy.

  • Familiar vanilla crafting flow
  • Improves item renewability in long-running worlds
  • Focused alternative to a large recipe engine
  • Easy to understand for new players

Administration

Configuration and live-server use

The plugin should be treated as a deliberate balance tool: expensive recipes can preserve exploration value, while cheaper recipes can solve scarcity on smaller servers. Legacy compatibility should be tested before production use.
