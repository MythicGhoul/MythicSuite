Overview

What Auto Breeding adds

Auto Breeding removes repetitive manual feeding from established farms while keeping the process tied to survival resources. Animals locate and consume nearby food, then enter breeding mode under configurable conditions.

Survival servers, farming communities and players who maintain large animal pens.
