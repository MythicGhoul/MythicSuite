Full overview

Let animals breed naturally using nearby food sources.

Auto Breeding lets established animal farms continue producing without requiring players to manually feed every pair. The system still consumes real survival resources, preserving the cost of breeding while removing repetitive clicking.

Animals search for appropriate nearby food under configurable conditions. Limits and timing controls prevent the automation from turning a small farm into uncontrolled entity growth.

Best suited toSurvival servers that allow farms but want breeding to remain resource-based and manageable.

How it works

Survival resource consumption with less repetitive interaction

Supported animals periodically look for valid food in the configured area. When requirements are met, the food is consumed and the animals enter the normal breeding state.

Species-aware handling keeps each animal tied to the correct resource. Server owners decide the search range, intervals, limits and other controls needed to fit the local economy and performance goals.

  • Maintains survival resource costs
  • Reduces repetitive farm maintenance
  • Configurable timing and population controls
  • Works as a focused utility rather than a full farming overhaul

Administration

Configuration and live-server use

The plugin is intentionally lightweight. Configuration centres on supported animals, food behaviour, timing, limits and range so administrators can balance convenience against entity count.
