Overview

What LevelCraft adds

LevelCraft transforms a survival server into a connected RPG world. Skills, ranks, rarity equipment, magic, bosses, questlines, prestige, souls, scaling enemies and world threats all feed into one long-term progression loop.

Long-term survival RPG communities that want every major system to connect.
