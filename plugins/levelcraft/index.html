Full overview

A complete RPG progression ecosystem built around survival gameplay.

LevelCraft is the progression backbone of GhoulCraft: a private RPG suite that connects skills, ranks, equipment power, magic, quests, bosses, world threats and prestige into one survival ecosystem.

Its systems are not isolated menus. Mining, combat, farming, exploration, crafting and server events feed the same long-term character journey, giving ordinary survival actions permanent value.

Best suited toGhoulCraft's long-term survival RPG experience; the project is not currently distributed as a public resource.

How it works

A connected progression loop from first login to prestige

Players develop multiple skills, unlock milestones and build enough overall strength to advance through the F-to-S rank structure. Rank paths give characters stronger identities rather than treating every build as identical.

Equipment rarity, power levels, sockets, gems, runes, wands and custom crafting expand the item layer. Bosses, corruption cores, questlines and events then give those upgrades meaningful challenges to overcome.

  • One progression language across most server activities
  • Long-term ranks, skills and prestige goals
  • Connected equipment, magic, quest and boss systems
  • Private design tailored directly to the GhoulCraft economy and world

Administration

Configuration and live-server use

LevelCraft includes configurable progression values, extensive custom content, integrations and administrative tools. Vault, PlaceholderAPI, Dynmap and Discord hooks allow the suite to share information with the rest of the server rather than operating as a closed system.
