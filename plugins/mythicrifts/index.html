Full overview

Corruption cores that spread danger and reshape the world around them.

MythicRifts creates persistent corruption outbreaks that make the world feel active even when players are not following a traditional quest. Cores appear as escalating regional threats with guardians, visual pressure and server-wide reporting.

The system is built around controlled spread rather than indiscriminate terrain replacement. Players have time to discover, organise and eliminate the threat before it reaches stronger stages.

Best suited toGhoulCraft's private living-world design, where threats should develop independently of normal quests.

How it works

Discover the outbreak, fight its guardians and stop the spread

A corruption core establishes itself in an eligible region and begins progressing through configured levels. Each stage can increase environmental effects, danger and guardian strength.

Players track active outbreaks through world information, map hooks and physical signs. Defeating the core or guardian resolves the event and connects back into LevelCraft quests and rewards.

  • Persistent world pressure outside scheduled events
  • Escalating difficulty with visible progression
  • Map, sign and Discord awareness
  • Claim-aware placement and controlled terrain effects

Administration

Configuration and live-server use

Administrators control eligibility, spread rules, level timing, guardians and notification behaviour. Clan-claim awareness prevents outbreaks from ignoring protected community areas.
